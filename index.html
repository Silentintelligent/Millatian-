Our mission

Aircraft that think, fly and report on their own.

Ibn Burhan designs and builds unmanned aircraft for long-endurance observation and defence. We develop airframes, flight software and sensor integration together, so every system is tested as one machine instead of a pile of parts.

Capabilities

Built around the mission, not the brochure.

Persistent ISR

Intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance from a single sortie, with payload cueing and live downlink.

Secure data links

Encrypted line-of-sight and beyond-line-of-sight communication with automatic failover.

Redundant flight control

Triple-redundant avionics and automatic return-to-base when a sensor or link degrades.

Terrain-aware navigation

Operation in mountains, coast and sea, including GPS-degraded conditions.

Ground control station

One operator interface for mission planning, live control and post-flight analysis.

Long endurance

Efficient airframes and propulsion tuned for many hours on station.

Roadmap

From design bench to field operation.

Stage 1

Design

Aerodynamics, structure and avionics modelled and simulated before any metal is cut.

Stage 2

Prototype

Scaled and full-size airframes built in-house with integrated flight software.

Stage 3

Flight test

Envelope expansion, payload trials and endurance flights with full data logging.

Stage 4

Field service

Production, operator training and long-term support for deployed fleets.
